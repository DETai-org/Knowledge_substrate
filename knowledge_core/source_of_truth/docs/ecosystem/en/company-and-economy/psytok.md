# PsyTok

**PsyTok is the unit used to price services within the DETai ecosystem.** It brings usage across different applications into a single, understandable balance. The unit name is **PsyTok**, regardless of quantity.

> **Status:** this page describes the PsyTok model. Support is introduced gradually. It does not announce availability in every application; the product shows available actions, current prices and terms before confirmation.

## One unit, different services

Audiobook preparation, personal podcasts and other paid actions can be priced in PsyTok. Future uses may include searching materials and working with intelligent assistants.

PsyTok does not represent a fixed number of model tokens, characters or seconds of computation. Each service has its own tariff, for example by text length or per action. Prices may be fractional. Changing the model or execution method does not change the balance's identity or ownership.

## Shared balance and included allowances

The balance belongs to a **DETai Account**. Purchased PsyTok are intended for supported applications; account and DETai Hub views are planned for the shared balance.

| Source | Where it can be used | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchased PsyTok | Supported ecosystem applications | Do not expire when a subscription ends |
| PsyTok included in a product plan | The product and services specified by that plan | Until the end of the relevant subscription period; do not roll over |

In Psychology in Quotes, included allowances cover the combined product: the bot and ΨTunes. When an action accepts both sources, eligible included credits are spent first, then purchased PsyTok. A shared unit does not remove the limits of a particular allowance.

Package sizes, top-up options and eligible services are defined by the current offer. A PsyTok balance does not itself replace a subscription, content access or permission to perform an action.

## Quotes and fulfilment

Before confirmation, the user sees the ordered result, price and material terms. Where direct payment such as Telegram Stars is available, a separate top-up is unnecessary: the related credit purchase, reservation and charge appear in the history.

The required amount may be reserved during fulfilment. Final charges follow the delivered order and accepted terms; unused reservations are released. Partial delivery is charged only under rules shown beforehand. Duplicate payment or completion notifications must not cause duplicate charges.

Internal recovery attempts do not create charges beyond the agreed order. New prices apply to new offers; confirmed orders retain their agreed terms.

## Books and prepared materials

In [ΨTunes](../technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes/psi-tunes/index.md), **preparing a material** and **acquiring permanent access to a ready book** are separate offers and may have different prices.

Fulfilment may use new preparation or an existing compatible version that may lawfully be supplied to the user. Matching title and author alone is insufficient: the text, language, translation and audio settings must fit. Changes to material order terms require the user's agreement.

Permanent access covers the purchased book in its specified language variant. Technical corrections to that variant do not require another purchase. A different book or language follows the terms of a separate offer.

Personal uploads do not automatically become public catalogue content. Reuse across users requires the appropriate permission; a private source or personal voice does not become shared merely because the book matches.
