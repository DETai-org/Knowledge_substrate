# Getting started with Psychology in Quotes

**Who it is for:** readers of psychology books, psychologists, students, educators and authors who work with quotations and sources.

**Start here:** [Telegram](https://t.me/DET_psy_in_quotes_bot) · [Website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes).

## 1. Explore first — you can create an Account later

Open the [Telegram bot](https://t.me/DET_psy_in_quotes_bot) and select `/start`, or begin with the [public web catalogue](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes). **You do not need a DETai Account** to explore published quotes, their sources or prepared translations. Watermarks on public images are a normal part of the preview experience.

**Public guest mode:** favourites and a temporary personal watermarked card, with limits on attempts and retention. You may be able to try again once the cycle ends. These flows were implemented in [PiQ PR #37](https://github.com/DETai-org/psychology-in-quotes/pull/37); check the running bot for the current guest restrictions. See the [live Free / Plus / Pro comparison on the website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans).

**When you need a DETai Account:** to retain your own creations beyond the guest expiry date, obtain a clean PNG, use a permanent collection, create Stories and publish through PiQ. An Account is free at the Free tier; Plus/Pro govern extended capabilities.

1. In PiQ, choose **“Open DETai Hub”** when prompted to connect an Account. Opening Hub alone neither registers you nor links accounts.
2. If Telegram is already linked to a DETai Account, Hub will display your existing account. Otherwise, choose either to **create an Account inside Hub** or **connect one you already created on the website**.
3. To use an existing account, sign in to the [DETai account area](https://detai-x.com/en/account), choose “Connect Telegram”, and confirm the link using a secure one-time Hub URL. Signing in on the website alone is not sufficient; there is no need to create a second Account.
4. Return to PiQ. **Your next normal action automatically checks the connection**, after which eligible guest data may be transferred to the permanent collection. “Check connection” can refresh the screen immediately, but is optional. Then open “Settings” → “My subscription”: a free Account grants Free, not Plus or Pro.

On the first handoff, Hub may receive **PiQ's interface language**. If Hub already has a saved language preference, that preference takes precedence. This does not change the language of a quote, its translation or the Account's profile language.

**Public means a guest; Free means a participant with a persistent shared identity; Plus/Pro mean participants with expanded plan capabilities.** These are interaction levels, not proof of professional status or consent to marketing messages.

[Current plans, features and limits on the website →](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans)

## 2. Create your own quote

In the bot, select **“✨ Create quote”**. Add the quote text, author, source title and, when available, the page number and edition. Check the language of the submitted passage and the design. Follow the creation wizard until saving is confirmed.

Your new quote appears under **“📚 My collection” → “✍️ My quotes”**, even if it has not been published in the public catalogue. For guests, this is a **temporary, unverified result** that cannot enter the public catalogue without a verified Account and the required review. For Account holders, a ready, clean image of their own quote does not consume an obtain quota, even on Free.

If the flow is interrupted, use the offered option to continue the existing draft. Guests have only one active project with limited retention; the bot shows its exact expiry. Do not make a duplicate “just in case”. If the current PNG is missing, see [the troubleshooting guide](help-and-troubleshooting.md#piq-image-not-ready).

## 3. Find and obtain somebody else's quote

1. Open the **“🌐 Catalogue”** on the website or in the bot.
2. Find a quote and read it together with the author and source. A prepared translation may be read as a **watermarked preview**.
3. **“⭐ Favorites”** saves only a bookmark, not a clean file. Check guest-favourite limits in the bot.
4. **“✨ Acquire quote”** is a separate confirmed operation, subject to a weekly limit; it associates **one quote (`quoteId`)** with your Account.
5. Open **“📚 My collection” → “📚 My acquired quotes”**, choose an authorised language and download the clean image if needed.

On **Free**, obtaining a quote records one selected language. **Plus/Pro** provide access to all prepared, permitted language editions of an obtained quote without another obtain charge. Re-downloading an allowed file or choosing standard styling does not use another obtain.

## 4. Publish a quote or create Stories

First, link Telegram to your DETai Account. For your own quote or a previously obtained one, choose publishing or Stories. **You must first obtain another person's quote before publishing it**; this is not required for your own work. Then select the prepared edition, a connected channel and confirm the action. Publishing and Stories have **separate limits**.

A send operation may be `pending`, `failed` or `uncertain`. If the result is unconfirmed, **do not blindly press Send again**. Open **“My publications”** and check the result.
