# PiQ collections, languages and plans

**Updated:** 30 October 2026. This page explains collections and access levels, **but does not reproduce the pricing or quota matrix**. Current Free / Plus / Pro features and quotas are defined by PiQ policy and displayed in the [live plan comparison on the official website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans). Check “My subscription” for your remaining allowances, additional entitlements and active plan.

## Guest, participant and subscriber are different states

- **Public:** a visitor without a shared DETai Account. Can browse the catalogue and watermarked translations, keep a small guest favourites collection and try a temporary quote card — where these functions are already available in the particular bot version.
- **Free:** a participant with a free DETai Account, a permanent personal PiQ workspace and baseline entitlements and limits. The Telegram-to-Account link must be verified separately.
- **Plus / Pro:** participants with extended entitlements for this particular product. They are **the same ecosystem participants**, not a separate membership category. You do not need to upgrade from Free to belong to DETai's shared user space.

**Explore first; create an Account when needed.** [Compare Free / Plus / Pro on the website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans). Opening the account area or linking an account does not subscribe you to marketing or publish your private quotes without your intention.

**About guest mode:** favourites, a temporary card and repeat cycles were implemented in [PiQ PR #37](https://github.com/DETai-org/psychology-in-quotes/pull/37). The product itself shows the current limits; merging a PR does not prove that its functionality is deployed to a particular bot.

## Terms worth distinguishing {#piq-collection-terms}

- **My quotes** — records you created, regardless of whether they have passed public moderation. As their owner, you can access the current prepared PNGs without separately obtaining your own quotes.
- **Obtained** — quotes by others from the catalogue associated with your DETai Account after confirmation.
- **Favorites** — bookmarks. They neither remove watermarks nor grant download rights.
- **My publications** — the history of actual sends through PiQ. Creating Stories does not itself mean that anything was published externally.
- **Obtaining** — occurs once per Account and **`quoteId`**, not once per PNG, language, revision or design template.
- **Downloading** — retrieves an authorised file; it is not, by itself, another purchase or obtain operation.
- **Preview** — a watermarked image for browsing.

## Current plans and limits

**[View the current Free / Plus / Pro comparison on the PiQ website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans).**

The website obtains the live product policy through its API. This document intentionally contains **no static numerical limits** for obtaining quotes, publishing, Stories, languages, channels, captions, BrandKit or other plan features; otherwise, the public guide could become inconsistent with the application.

Check [“My subscription”](https://detai-x.com/en/account/products/psychology-in-quotes#piq-access) for your own entitlements and quotas. Usage operations may be reserved until their outcomes are confirmed, so the application is the source of truth for your remaining balance. Obtaining another person's quote, publishing and creating Stories are distinct operations. Creating your own quote is not the same as obtaining somebody else's.

## Languages and subscription changes

- In the catalogue, prepared and published translations can be viewed **for reference** with watermarks.
- When you obtain another person's quote, the entitlement is recorded against your Account. The languages eligible for clean files depend on your active entitlements and the [website policy](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans).
- Downloading an already authorised file again does not obtain the quote again. Only prepared and permitted editions are available.
- When you downgrade, an obtained quote stays in your collection. Language entitlements already confirmed are retained according to policy; access to new additional languages is governed by your current plan.
- PiQ cannot physically revoke files already saved on your device. Deleting or withdrawing a work can, however, restrict future delivery from the service.
- Changing the **interface language** does not change quote entitlements.

**There are three different languages:** the language of the submitted passage, the language of the original work (when known) and the language of the displayed edition. Do not infer the original book's language merely from the author's nationality.

## Availability and price

PiQ defines Free, Plus and Pro access levels and corresponding server-side entitlements, but **Telegram Stars are available only in the closed pilot; public self-service payments are not enabled**. Describing plans is not an offer to buy a subscription at a stated price. Where checkout is available, the price, currency, period, auto-renewal, cancellation and refund process must be shown **before payment confirmation**. The applicable offer and [Terms](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes/terms) determine the exact conditions.

## Legal note

Obtaining a quote grants the **right to use eligible PiQ functions**, not copyright ownership of a literary work, someone else's translation or a book. Respect attribution, third-party rights and publishing terms. A work with disputed provenance may become unavailable.
