# Psychology in Quotes

**Every quote is more than an image.**

**Psychology in Quotes (PiQ)** is a digital **educational and knowledge-sharing product** of [Platform DETai](../../index.md), part of the [DETai ecosystem](../../../index.md). It helps people explore psychological ideas, preserve meaningful passages from books together with their authors, sources and context, discover other people's insights, and build a personal collection of knowledge.

The DETai ecosystem brings together psychotherapy, research, education, technology and community to advance psychotherapy in the twenty-first century. **Platform DETai** connects digital products, accounts and technological capabilities. **PiQ brings together self-directed learning, reading culture and digital creativity:** people can select, reflect on, save and share meaningful quotations.

**Primary user function:** education and public knowledge-sharing. **Additional functions:** publishing and digital creativity. **Role in the ecosystem:** E2 Brand — spreading psychological knowledge, building awareness and trust in DETai. These are independent dimensions. PiQ is not a formal education programme with a curriculum or certification, nor is it psychotherapy, diagnosis or medical care.

## Get started in one minute

1. Open the [product page](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes) or the [Telegram bot](https://t.me/DET_psy_in_quotes_bot). **No registration is needed to explore.**
2. Browse the public catalogue, prepared translations, quotation records and watermarked images.
3. Guest mode provides a small favourites collection and a temporary quote card with a limited number of renders. This flow is implemented in the product code; check the bot interface to see whether it is available in your deployed version.
4. To save your work permanently, obtain an unwatermarked image or publish through PiQ, open **DETai Hub** from the bot. It will offer to create a free DETai Account or connect Telegram to an existing account. If the connection already exists, it will show your account. Signing in on the website alone does not link Telegram for bot operations.

## From visitor to ecosystem participant

**Public — a DETai guest:** anyone may explore open knowledge and products without creating a shared account. PiQ uses Telegram identity and limited temporary state for this purpose. A guest is a legitimate visitor, not an authentication error.

**Free — a participant with a DETai Account:** an enduring identity and personal workspace, including a permanent collection, a link between the website and Telegram, personal materials, entitlements and Free-tier limits. This is more than a technical sign-in: it lets people continue their work and participate in a growing ecosystem linking psychology, research, education and contemporary AI tools.

**Plus / Pro — a participant with extended product access:** the same DETai Account, with additional PiQ features, limits and tools under an actually active subscription. A subscription is not an administrator role or separate organisational membership.

These are **levels of interaction with the ecosystem**, not judgements about personality, professional qualifications or beliefs. Registration does not imply consent to marketing, community obligations or an automatically granted paid subscription. The value of participation lies in continuity, a common identity, the ability to retain results and the freedom to engage meaningfully in projects of one's choice.

The same approach is gradually being adopted by other DETai products: **first experience the value, then obtain a persistent account and entitlements if needed**. In PiQ, actual functions and restrictions are determined by its own policy, not by a broad notion of “membership”.

## Features and access

Psychology in Quotes works in Telegram and on the Platform DETai website. Quote creation and moderation, the catalogue, favourites, “My collection”, prepared language editions, publishing and Stories are available according to **Free / Plus / Pro** entitlements and the current product policy. Plus and Pro subscriptions are purchased with Telegram Stars; checkout requires a completed DETai Account linked to Telegram. Check current guest limits in the bot and [current offers, prices and plan limits on the website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans). The running product shows whether purchasing and individual features are available.

The [collection and plans guide](collection-and-plans.md) explains entitlements; **current numerical limits are published only in the [website's plan comparison](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes#piq-plans)**. Your effective access is determined by **DETai Account and PiQ server policy**, not an outdated copied table.
