# Podcasts in ΨTunes

[← ΨTunes](index.md)

A **podcast** is a standalone material with a title, text edition and possibly an audio version. Episodes can form a series while retaining their own position and identity.

> **Status:** podcast catalogue and personal preparation features are being developed. Availability, quotas and pricing are determined by the live product after release.

**DETai podcasts** are editorial episodes; inclusion in a series does not automatically publish an episode. A **personal podcast** starts from a text you have permission to use. Import and audio preparation are separate actions. Preparation is paid in [PsyTok](../../../../company-and-economy/psytok.md), using eligible included allowances or purchased credits. A personal episode is limited to 15,000 characters; this is a text limit, not a fixed number of free episodes per month. Eligibility and current prices are shown before ordering.

A series helps listeners explore a topic in order. Translation and renaming must not redirect bookmarks to a different work. Adding a material to a collection is not the same as acquiring listening rights. Sharing or exchanging personal content is unavailable unless explicitly enabled by the product.
