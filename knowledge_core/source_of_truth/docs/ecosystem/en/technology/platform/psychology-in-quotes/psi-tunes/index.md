# ΨTunes — books and podcasts

**ΨTunes** is an upcoming part of [Psychology in Quotes](../index.md) bringing reading, listening, bookmarks and source-linked quotations together.

> **Status:** ΨTunes 2.0 is under development and device testing. This guide does **not** announce a public launch or promise that every feature is currently enabled in the bot. The live interface determines available actions and permissions.

A book or podcast may have a text edition and an audio version. The player aims to preserve your position, let you revisit a moment and move from a quotation back to its source. A prepared audio file and permission to listen to it are separate conditions.

## Explore

- [Import a personal document](import.md) — source formats, checking chapters and preparing narration.
- [Podcasts and series](podcasts.md) — editorial and personal episodes, collections and access.
- [Listening and bookmarks](listening.md) — player controls, connection interruptions, restoring a position and permissions.

Personal features use your DETai Account connected to Telegram. Importing text does not automatically place an order or make a book public. Current plans and access conditions are shown on the [official PiQ website](https://detai-x.com/en/technology/platform/psychology-in-quotes). ΨTunes is for working with knowledge, not therapy, diagnosis or medical care.

Paid services use [PsyTok](../../../../company-and-economy/psytok.md). Included plan allowances and purchased shared credits are tracked separately. The user purchases an agreed result and access to the selected variant. Pricing follows the offer terms; using an existing compatible version does not itself change the agreed price. Permanent access covers the purchased language variant; technical corrections do not require a new purchase.

## Gifts {#gifts}

In the developing ΨTunes gifting flow, **Pro** lets you give an eligible book you purchased or uploaded. Eligibility is checked for the selected version and language. You keep your access, and the recipient receives permanent access to that version. Subscription-only access does not make a book eligible for gifting; a personal upload is not published in the shared catalog.

Open the book and choose “Give a book”. Enter the recipient’s **DETai Account username**, not their Telegram username. If you do not know it, or the person has not completed registration, choose **“Give via a link”**. After a separate confirmation, a one-use link is created. The first other user to activate it receives the gift. Send the link privately to the intended recipient; full registration in advance is not required.

Reviewing terms does not spend PsyTok. The gift price and allowance are used when you confirm the gift, **including when a link is created**, not when the recipient opens it. The link can be copied again from gift history. Book access and the congratulatory message may arrive at different times.

### Gift allowance and restoration {#gift-limits}

The current Pro model provides **one gift allowance per period**. This does not mean free delivery: arranging the gift is charged separately in PsyTok.

The counter shows the **used**, not remaining, allowance:

| Display | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Used: **0 / 1** | You can arrange a gift; its price requires separate confirmation. |
| Used: **1 / 1** | The allowance is used. Restore it for a fee or wait for your next Pro period. |

**“Reset allowance”** first opens the restoration terms. Restoration and the next gift are **two separate paid actions**. For example, a restoration price of 30 PsyTok and a gift price of 15 PsyTok mean a total of 45 PsyTok: confirm the 30 first, then the 15 separately. These are example amounts; the application shows the current price before each confirmation.

After confirmed restoration, the counter returns to **0 / 1**. No book is sent automatically: continue, choose the recipient and confirm the gift. Resetting does not extend Pro or carry an allowance into another period. A new allowance without a separate restoration fee becomes available when the next Pro period begins; the current period’s end date alone does not promise subscription renewal.

An insufficient balance prevents confirmation. If the payment response is missing, refresh the status and check history before arranging another payment. See [PsyTok](../../../../company-and-economy/psytok.md) for general credit and spending rules.
